Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the ₱185,000 price?

Roundtrip Saudia Airlines ticket, Umrah visa, Rawdah permit, travel tax, KSA health insurance, hotels in Makkah and Madinah, and Ziyarat in Madinah, Makkah and Taif — plus a free pilgrim kit.

How close are the hotels to the Haram?

Our Makkah hotel is about 5–8 minutes from Masjid Al-Haram, and our Madinah hotel is about 5 minutes from Al-Masjid An-Nabawi.

Do you assist with the Umrah visa?

Yes. The Umrah visa and Rawdah permit are part of the package, and our team guides you on every requirement.

Can I book for my family or a group?

Yes. Message us with the number of pilgrims and we'll prepare the details for your group.

How do I reserve a slot?

Message us on WhatsApp (+63 938 339 5849), Messenger, or our contact form and we'll walk you through the reservation.

What is Badal Umrah?

Badal Umrah is Umrah performed on behalf of a loved one who has passed away, or who can no longer travel because of age or illness. Message us and we'll explain the process and requirements.

Can you help with Nusuk registration?

Yes. We help you register on Nusuk, Saudi Arabia's official platform, and book your Umrah and Rawdah permits.

What's the difference between Economy and VIP transportation?

Economy uses shared group transport, while VIP gives you a private vehicle for you and your family. Both cover airport transfers, Makkah–Madinah travel and Ziyarah trips.

Do you offer customized or VIP Umrah packages?

Yes. Tell us your preferred dates, budget and hotel preferences, and we'll prepare a package and quotation for you.

Is Qasim Travel & Tours licensed?

Yes. We hold Ministry of Hajj & Umrah Licence No. 1007955, DTI Business Name No. 7354864 and Siniloan Business Permit No. 2026-0403429000-0919.
